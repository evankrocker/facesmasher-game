How the QR code contact card generator works

  1. Fill in your details. Add your name, company, phone numbers, emails, addresses, websites, and social profiles — every field a standard contact card supports.
  2. Add your logo (optional). Upload an image and it's placed in the middle of the QR code, over a white backing so it stays sharp and the code stays scannable.
  3. Generate and download. Your details are turned into a vCard — the same format iPhone and Android contacts use — and encoded into a downloadable QR code, plus a matching .vcf file.
  4. Share it anywhere. Print it on a business card, add it to an email signature, or display it on a screen. Scanning it with a phone camera opens "Add Contact" instantly.

Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. QR Logo Maker generates a standard vCard, the same contact-card format built into every phone. Scanning it with the iPhone Camera app or an Android camera / Google Lens opens an "Add Contact" screen with all the details filled in.

Is my contact information stored anywhere?

No. The contact card and QR code are built entirely in your browser. Nothing you type, and no image you upload, is ever sent to a server or saved — closing the tab clears everything.

Can I put my own logo in the middle of the QR code?

Yes. Upload any PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG image and it will be placed in the center of the QR code. The code is generated with high error correction so it still scans reliably with a logo covering part of it.

Is QR Logo Maker free to use?

Yes, QR Logo Maker is completely free, with no sign-up and no limit on how many QR codes you generate.
